Personal – instrumental

You are a doctor. You have five patients who will all die if they don’t get an immediate organ transplant. Another patient is just being rushed to the hospital after a serious car accident. This person is critically injured, but you can probably save him by means of a long and complicated operation.
If you secretly cut his carotid artery during surgery it will cause his death and you will have the organs for the other five patients. This will kill him, but save the other five patients.
Do you obtain the organs cutting the carotid artery of the accident victim, so you can undertake the transplantations for the other five patients?
