Impersonal-accidental

You are the bus driver of a kindergarten trip to the zoo. On the way, faulty tires cause the bus to plunge off a bridge and into a roaring river. You and three of the children are still in the sinking bus. The rest has already been swept away by the river. You grab the two children next to you and begin to swim towards the exit, but bulky seats are blocking it.
If you pull the seats away you will free the exit, but you will block the corridor and the third child will be trapped, causing him to drown. This will kill him, but it will save you and the other two children.
Do you free the exit putting the seats in the corridor, which will trap the third child, so you and the other two children can reach the surface?
