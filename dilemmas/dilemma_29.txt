Personal – accidental

A viral epidemic has spread across the globe killing thousands of people. You are a medical researcher and have developed two substances in your laboratory. Due to a mistake both are labeled as vaccine. One of them is indeed the vaccine, which could save thousands of lives. The other one is lethal.
If you inject the substances to your two lab assistants without knowing which substance is which, you will be able to identify the vaccine. One of the assistants will die, but you will be able to save thousands of lives with your vaccine.
Do you identify the vaccine injecting these two substances into your two lab assistants which will poison one of them, so thousands of people can be vaccinated?
